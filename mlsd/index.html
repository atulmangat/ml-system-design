How to Practice With This Guide

Strategies for self-study and mock interviews
  1. 1
    Master Chapter 1 first

    It defines the 7-step blueprint, the latency budgeting method, and the funnel rubric every answer follows.

  2. 2
    Read only the requirements

    For each problem, read “Clarifying Requirements” and close the page.

  3. 3
    Sketch your own design

    Take 10 minutes on paper to outline the ML objective, features, baseline model, and metrics.

  4. 4
    Compare with the chapter

    Review the worked diagrams and notice where your design had gaps or missed operational trade-offs.

  5. 5
    Test yourself out loud

    Each chapter ends with Senior, Staff and Principal level questions. Filter by level, hit “Drill me” for a timed two-minute question, answer aloud, then reveal. Rate yourself on the scorecard in the Introduction to see which axis to study next.
